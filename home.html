Save your BrainZ!!!

Put on your helmets and save your brainz!!!

The Zombie apocalypse is finally here and it's up to you to preserve that brain of yours. You better keep it safe because someone needs to pass on the knowledge to the future generations! So put on your thinking cap and get ready to exterminate the living dead.

Instructions: Use the up and down arrow keys to move along the trench, and the spacebar to shoot. Need more ammo? Answer the questions correctly and as fast as you can to get more ammo. The faster you answer, the more ammo you get. If you answer correctly but take too long you get less ammo. And if you answer incorrectly, you get no ammo at all. So be quick and keep your brain safe. Good luck and have fun!
